Why this approach

Marks and attendance describe the past, not your future in a career. Business, design and sales roles reward communication, experience and initiative, so CareerCompass uses grades with a small, career-specific weight and judges your future by skills, interests, experience and priorities.

Pipeline

Profile→Skill and experience scoring→RIASEC interest map→Career matching→Readiness forecast→90-day roadmap

Formulas

Career match

(1−g)·(0.45·Skills + 0.25·Interest + 0.15·Experience + 0.15·Priorities) + g·Grades, where g is 0–10% depending on the career (e.g. 0% for entrepreneur, 10% for ML engineer).

Readiness

0.55·SkillCoverage + 0.30·Experience + 0.15·InterestFit for the top career.

Time to job-ready

Hours needed = 40 per missing skill + experience gap, divided by your weekly hours. Readiness of 75+ is "job-ready".

Limitations & future scope

  • Weights are expert-defined; future versions can learn them from placement data
  • Career list covers 19 common paths and can be extended in the data table
  • Future: live job-market trends, mentor matching, resume analysis, multi-language support
